Export Data

What you can export: Products, Customers, Inventory, Categories, Orders, Returns, and Refunds. CSV files open in any spreadsheet app. Excel XLSX requires openpyxl on the server.

  • All monetary values are in VND (whole numbers).
  • Orders export one row per order (summary only — no line-item detail).
  • Importing back is supported for: Categories, Products, Customers, and Inventory. Go to Import to re-import edited exports.
  • Returns, Refunds, and Orders cannot be re-imported (too many business rules to replay safely).
